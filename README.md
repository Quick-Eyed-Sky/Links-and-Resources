# Links & Resources

A small hub pointing to my main creative projects online.

## 📝 A French Toolbox

**[afrenchtoolbox.wordpress.com](https://afrenchtoolbox.wordpress.com/)**

A blog exploring creative patterns across disciplines — how composers, painters, filmmakers, and now AI image generators solve similar problems with very different tools. Posts range from musical modulation techniques and Renaissance painting analysis to AI art experimentation and cultural curiosities, all written with a curious, playful, "process over perfection" spirit — deliberately, cheerfully in imperfect English.

## 🎬 Quick-Eyed Sky (YouTube)

**[youtube.com/@QuickEyedSky](https://www.youtube.com/@QuickEyedSky)**

AI-made images, clips, and tutorials focused on Stable Diffusion and AI art techniques — practical, hands-on guidance for anyone getting into AI-generated visuals. Companion content (advanced tips and prompts) is also shared via Patreon.

## 🎱 Prankster Pool

**[quick-eyed-sky.github.io/prankster-pool](https://quick-eyed-sky.github.io/prankster-pool/)**

A pool table in the browser that looks perfectly normal, until it isn't. Twenty-two switchable "mutations" — pockets that shy away, a referee who swears nothing happened, a VCR that rewinds your best shot — and a *Prank a friend* button that sends someone an ordinary-looking table whose pranks start on their second shot. Also on [itch.io](https://quick-eyed-sky.itch.io/prankster-pool); the code lives in [prankster-pool](https://github.com/Quick-Eyed-Sky/prankster-pool).
